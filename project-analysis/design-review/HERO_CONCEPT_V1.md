# Hero Concept V1

- **Font direction:** Courier Prime — mechanical, readable, research-terminal character.
- **Palette:** near-black and off-white with muted gray; ice cyan used only as a system signal.
- **Positioning:** Creative Destruction as an AI, software, digital product and R&D company; AI Governance remains one initiative within the wider portfolio.
- **Composition:** headline and transformation field form one continuous desktop composition.
- **Metaphor:** fragmented legacy structures converge through a transition core and resolve into an emergent technical system.
